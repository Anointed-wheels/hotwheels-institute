Do I need a personal laptop before registering for classes?

+
While having a personal laptop is highly recommended for practicing outside of class hours and coding at home, our physical hub is equipped with modern technical developer machines for students to utilize during dedicated lab training hours.

What schedules are available for working professionals?

+
We operate flexible scheduling modules. We feature intensive weekday pipelines alongside robust weekend-only immersion tracks tailored specifically for corporate workers, university students, and busy professionals.

Does HCTI provide flexible tuition payment installments?

+
Yes, absolutely. To make high-end tech education highly accessible, we offer structured tuition payment models allowing students to split their overall course fees across manageable monthly installments over the training period.
